# Verified Research & Claims Log — 00-test-30s (The Blood-Brain Barrier)

| Claim ID | Exact Verified Fact | Primary Source URL | Checked Date | Strict Scope (What Source Actually Proves) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `C1` | Cerebral endothelial cells linked by tight junctions form the blood-brain barrier (BBB), sealing microvessels and blocking passive paracellular diffusion of proteins and polar solutes. | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK519556/ | 2026-11-23 | Applies to CNS microvessels (excluding circumventricular organs). |
| `C2` | More than 90% of small-molecule drugs and nearly 100% of larger therapeutics cannot cross the BBB (Pardridge estimates >98% of small-molecule drugs). | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK519556/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2756824/ | 2026-11-23 | Safe to state "Over 90 percent of small drugs cannot cross this wall." |
| `C3` | Only very small/lipid-soluble molecules or molecules carried by specific transporters can cross the intact BBB. | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK519556/ | 2026-11-23 | Do not claim zero substances cross; nutrients use dedicated transporters. |

## Intentionally Excluded
- Avoid claiming "100% of all drugs fail" because small lipid-soluble CNS drugs (<400–500 Da) and transporter-mediated drugs do cross.
